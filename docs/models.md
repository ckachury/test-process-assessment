# The two models in brief

A short orientation, written for sponsors and teams before an assessment. For the full models, use the official publications.

## TMMi (Test Maturity Model integration)

A **staged** model: an organisation reaches a level when it satisfies all the process areas of that level and the ones below.

| Level | Name | Process areas |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial | None. Testing is ad hoc. |
| 2 | Managed | Test Policy and Strategy · Test Planning · Test Monitoring and Control · Test Design and Execution · Test Environment |
| 3 | Defined | Test Organization · Test Training Program · Test Lifecycle and Integration · Non-functional Testing · Peer Reviews |
| 4 | Measured | Test Measurement · Product Quality Evaluation · Advanced Reviews |
| 5 | Optimization | Defect Prevention · Quality Control · Test Process Optimization |

**How I use it:** as a self-assessment questionnaire scored 1 to 5 per area, for a quick and comparable picture across organisations. In my Master's research, I used it to compare 10 startups by growth stage ([summary](https://github.com/ckachury/lean-quality-framework)).

## TPI NEXT

A **continuous** model: each key area matures on its own, so the organisation can improve where the business needs it most.

- **16 key areas** in three groups.
- **Four maturity levels** per key area: Initial, Controlled, Efficient and Optimizing. Each level is defined by checkpoints.
- **Clusters** group checkpoints from different key areas into a suggested improvement order, which can be adjusted to the business drivers.
- **Enablers** link testing to other improvement models used in the organisation.

| Group | Key areas |
|---|---|
| Stakeholder relations | Stakeholder commitment · Degree of involvement · Test strategy · Test organization · Communication · Reporting |
| Test management | Test process management · Estimating and planning · Metrics · Defect management · Testware management |
| Test profession | Methodology practice · Tester professionalism · Test case design · Test tools · Test environment |

**How I use it:** for a corporate quality department, starting from a business drivers workshop and gathering evidence through documents, interviews and one workshop per group of key areas.

## Combining them

- Use TMMi when a recognised staged reference is needed, or to compare many organisations quickly.
- Use TPI NEXT when the goal is a prioritised improvement plan for one organisation.
- The evidence matrix and the workshops are the same for both. Only the mapping and scoring step changes.
