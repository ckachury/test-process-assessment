# Interview guide

45 to 60 minutes, one person at a time. Ask for examples, not opinions: *"Tell me about the last time…"* works better than *"Do you usually…?"*.

## Opening (5 min)

- Purpose of the assessment and how the information will be used.
- Nothing is attributed to the person; findings are reported in aggregate.
- Ask permission before recording or using a transcription tool. Notes are the default.

## Quality or test lead

1. Walk me through how a change moves from request to production. Where does testing come in?
2. How do you decide what to test, how much, and when to stop?
3. Tell me about the last release that went out with a known risk. Who decided, and with what information?
4. Which numbers do you look at every week? Which decision did one of them change recently?
5. How are estimates for testing made, and how do they compare with what actually happens?
6. What happens to test cases and scripts after a project ends?
7. How do new testers learn the job here?
8. If you could change one thing tomorrow without asking anyone, what would it be?

## Tester or test automation engineer

1. Tell me about a test you are proud of. What made it possible?
2. Which test design techniques do you use? Where did you learn them?
3. In a normal week, where does your time go that is not testing?
4. What happens when you find a defect? Who sets the priority, and can it change?
5. How stable and available are the test environments and test data?
6. Which tool could you not live without, and which would you drop tomorrow?
7. What must always be done for a test to be trustworthy?

## Development lead

1. When do testers get involved in your work?
2. What do you receive from testing, and when? What would you like to receive?
3. How do unit tests, code reviews and testing by QA fit together?
4. Tell me about the last defect that reached production. How was it found and why was it missed?

## Product owner or business representative

1. What does quality mean for your product and your users?
2. How do you take part in deciding what is tested and whether a release goes out?
3. What information do you get from testing? Does it help you decide?
4. What would make you trust a release more?

## Closing (5 min)

- Is there anything I should have asked and didn't?
- Who else should I talk to?
- Which documents or data would show what we discussed?
