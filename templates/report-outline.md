# Assessment report outline

A report written for two readers: the sponsor, who needs a decision in ten minutes, and the teams, who need to recognise their reality and know what changes.

## 1. Executive summary (1 page)

- Why the assessment was done, in terms of the business drivers.
- Current maturity in one picture (profile chart) and in three sentences.
- The three findings that matter most for the business.
- Recommended first steps and what they cost in time and people.

## 2. Scope and method

- Teams, systems and period covered; what was out of scope.
- Model used and why.
- Evidence sources with counts: documents reviewed, interviews, workshops and participants, tool data.
- Limitations: areas with low confidence, sources not available.

## 3. Business drivers and target profile

| Business driver | Signal of success | Areas that support it | Target level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fewer incidents after release | Incidents per release; defect leakage | Test strategy · Test case design · Defect management | Controlled → Efficient |

## 4. Current maturity profile

- One row per area: current rating, confidence, key evidence, gap to target.
- Strengths first. Teams need to see what already works.

## 5. Findings

For each finding:

- **What we observed**, with evidence references (e.g. EV-042, EV-057).
- **Why it matters** for a business driver.
- **Root cause**, when it is visible.
- **Areas affected.**

Group findings by root cause rather than by area when one cause explains several gaps.

## 6. Improvement roadmap

| Horizon | Improvement | Owner | Effect on driver | Effort | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick win (≤ 8 weeks) | | | | | |
| Next (3–6 months) | | | | | |
| Later (6–12 months) | | | | | |

- Quick wins come first and include ideas from the teams themselves.
- Dependencies between items are made explicit.
- State what will **stop** being done, not only what will start.

## 7. KPIs and follow-up

- Three to six KPIs, each tied to a business driver, with baseline, target and owner.
- Review cadence and date of the re-assessment.

## Annexes

- Evidence matrix (anonymised).
- Workshop summaries.
- Glossary for non-testing readers.

## Scoring conventions

- Ratings: met · partially met · not met · not assessed.
- Confidence: high · medium · low.
- Never present an area as mature or immature when its confidence is low; say what evidence is missing.
